# Demo video runbook

The repository cannot upload to YouTube without an authenticated creator
account. Record a 4:50 screen capture while running `docker compose up -d`,
then upload it as unlisted or public.

1. 0:00–0:20: pitch: “an AI tutor that shows its work”.
2. 0:20–1:00: upload PDF, slides, and video; show processing and the map.
3. 1:00–1:40: ask a cross-source question and open PDF, slide, and video citations.
4. 1:40–2:10: ask a diagram question and highlight the figure.
5. 2:10–2:30: ask an unsupported question and show refusal.
6. 2:30–3:30: onboard, take the diagnostic, answer Precision/Recall incorrectly twice,
   and open remediation.
7. 3:30–4:00: show live mastery bars and the reshuffled study plan.
8. 4:00–4:30: open `/evaluation` and show RAGAS, ablations, simulator plots, and limitations.
9. 4:30–4:50: show the architecture diagram and close with “Built in 5 days for hackathon”.
